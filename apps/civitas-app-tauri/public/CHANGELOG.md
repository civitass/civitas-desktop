## Consumer preview

This build prepares Civitas as a local-first personal work memory.

### New

- Bring your own OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Amazon Bedrock, local, or
  compatible model endpoint.
- Review evidence-backed Next Actions with confidence, uncertainty, safety
  state, and Done, Not now, or Dismiss feedback.
- Explore personal projects, people, decisions, reasons, blockers, and
  procedures through the local knowledge graph.
- Connect trusted MCP clients through an authenticated, scoped loopback bridge.

### Privacy and reliability

- Capture, indexes, graph data, feedback, and action history remain on the
  computer by default.
- Civitas no longer requires an account, hosted credit balance, Railway
  service, or enterprise control plane.
- Credentials use an OS-vault-backed encrypted store; session-only credentials
  disappear when Civitas quits.
- A fresh install remains paused and does not start capture or health polling
  until the user reaches the explicit engine-consent step.
- The consumer migration removes dormant non-agent knowledge-graph grants and
  constrains new grants to local AI agents.
- Remote model destinations are shown before activation, and suggestions never
  execute automatically.
