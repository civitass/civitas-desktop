# Community code of conduct

Civitas welcomes people of different backgrounds, identities, experience
levels, and perspectives. Participation should be safe, constructive, and
focused on the work.

## Expected

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith while accepting correction.
- Make space for newcomers and explain project-specific context.
- Protect personal, customer, captured, and security-sensitive information.
- Respect consent and boundaries in research, testing, and demonstrations.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal
  attacks;
- doxxing or sharing private communications or captured data;
- publishing credentials or vulnerability details through public channels;
- spam, manipulation, impersonation, or coordinated abuse;
- using project spaces to monitor or target another person.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and restrict
participation when needed to protect the community or users. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary
disclosure.

Before public launch, the repository owner must publish and test a private
conduct-reporting contact. Until then, use the existing private project channel
to contact repository owners.
